K I L N ●

Mint fully on-chain 1/1s on networked.art — nothing on IPFS. Two ways:

  1. Upload files. Your image and HTML become bytes in contract storage. Every byte is paid for at mint, so smaller files mean less gas — a few KB costs cents, a few hundred KB costs real money.
  2. Reference a vessel token. Point the poster, the animation, or both at content already stored on The Vessel and the token stores only a pointer — under 1 KB whatever the artwork weighs, because the bytes are already on-chain. Rendered at view time by VesselPortal, a renderer registered on your collection.
